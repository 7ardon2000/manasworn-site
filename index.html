An isometric dungeon crawl about casting — three spellcasters, generated depths, and no weapon but your own mana.

The game

Play a Mage, a Necromancer or a Shaman and descend through generated dungeons, cemeteries, temples and deserts. Every run rebuilds the world; every kill feeds a build you assemble from spells, relics, runes and runewords rather than from swords.

Bring up to three friends — Manasworn is drop-in four-player co-op.
