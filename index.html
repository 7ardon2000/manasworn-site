The game

Play a Mage, a Necromancer or a Shaman and descend through generated dungeons, cemeteries, temples and deserts. Every run rebuilds the world; every kill feeds a build made of spells, relics, runes and runewords rather than swords.

Mana is life

No blades, no shields. You are a caster, and your mana is the only thing between you and the dark.

Shape your spells

Relics, runes and runewords change what a spell does, not just how hard it hits.

Dungeons that keep going

Generated depths with bosses at the bottom and a difficulty ladder above them.

Bring friends

Drop-in co-op for up to four. Descend together.

Mage

Comets, novas and beams. Raw arcane, thrown from range.

Necromancer

Raise the dead, drain the living, let the pack do the work.

Shaman

Chained lightning and storms that fill a room.
